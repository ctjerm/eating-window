Eating Window is a timer for 16:8 intermittent fasting. Tap when you take your first bite. You get an 8-hour eating window, then the app follows the 16-hour fast after it, which is the part that counts.

<ul>
<li><b>Eating window.</b> A ring counts down 8 hours. "Last call" comes at 7 hours and "Kitchen's closed" at 8.</li>
<li><b>Fast.</b> A silent live countdown sits in the notification shade, and "Fast complete" arrives at 16 hours with a Start now button.</li>
<li><b>What your body is doing.</b> A short note for each stage of the fast, limited to what human studies show.</li>
<li><b>Streak of completed fasts.</b> A fast counts once it reaches 16 hours. Starting a window early asks first.</li>
<li><b>Corrections.</b> Set the time you really started, end a window early, or log a late snack, which restarts the fast from then.</li>
<li><b>Daily start nudge.</b> An optional reminder at your usual start time.</li>
<li><b>Quick Settings tile.</b> Shows when the window or fast ends. Once the fast is done, a tap starts the next window.</li>
<li><b>History.</b> The past week and your recent fasts.</li>
</ul>

There is no account, no ads and no internet permission. Your windows and fasts stay on your phone.

Reminders use Android's alarm clock, so they arrive on time even on phones that delay ordinary alarms. The cost is an alarm icon in the status bar.

This app is not medical advice. If you are pregnant, have diabetes, or have a history of eating disorders, check with a doctor before fasting.
